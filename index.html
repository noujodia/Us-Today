🛡️ Privacy & your data

What we store, who can see it, and how to delete it
▼

What Noudé stores

Names, nicknames, captions, photoYou two
About me answersYou two
Daily check-ins and notesYou two
Chat, photos, videos, stories, songsYou two
Invites, challenges, goals, dates, wish list linkYou two
Usage counts, time spent in the app and error reports (no content, no names)The app's owner
Feedback you choose to sendThe app's owner

Never collected: email, phone number, passwords, location, contacts or payment details.

How it's protected

  • Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest on Google's Firebase servers.
  • Your space can only be opened with your private personal link. Turn on the Privacy lock and a phone also needs your 6-digit passcode.
  • Notifications are encrypted, so Apple and Google can't read them on the way.
  • AI ideas are off unless you both turn them on. Then only check-ins and About me answers are used, never chat or photos, and the AI provider doesn't train on them.
  • Stories disappear after 24 hours. Photos and videos can auto-delete after 15 or 30 days.

Good to know

  • Your personal link is your key. Keep it private, and turn on the Privacy lock.
  • We never look at your content. It's against company policy to view any member's data, such as photos, videos, messages and check-ins. The only data collected is anonymous usage data, such as how many couples check in, which features get used and how long people spend in the app. Error reports and feedback you choose to send are the only other things we see.
  • A note for the beta. Your data is stored securely on Google's servers. While we're in beta, we recommend not sending explicit photos or videos.

Leave Noudé and delete my data

Permanently deletes your profile, answers, check-ins, messages, photos, videos, stories and goals. Your partner keeps only what they created themselves. If your partner has already left, the whole space is deleted. It can't be undone.
